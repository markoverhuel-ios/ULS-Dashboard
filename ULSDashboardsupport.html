What ULS Dashboard does

ULS Dashboard tracks FCC wireless licenses by FRN (FCC Registration Number). Add an FRN, and the app pulls in every license on file for it directly from the FCC's public ULS system, then organizes it by status, expiration, and location.

Frequently asked questions

Where does the license data come from?
Directly from the FCC's public Universal Licensing System (ULS). The app doesn't maintain its own database — it fetches live data and caches it on your device for offline viewing.

Can I file a license or renewal through the app?
No. ULS Dashboard is read-only. It does not submit anything to the FCC. For filings and renewals, use the FCC's own ULS site.

Is my data backed up anywhere?
No. All tracked FRNs, cached licenses, and settings are stored only on your device. There's no account and no server, so nothing syncs across devices and nothing is recoverable if the app is deleted — export your license list to CSV first if you need a copy.

Why is a license showing as "No Longer Active" but I still see it?
By default, expired/terminated/cancelled licenses stay visible so you have a full record. Go to Settings to auto-hide them after 30/90/180/365 days.

The app isn't affiliated with the FCC — what does that mean?
ULS Dashboard is an independent tool built on top of publicly available FCC data. It is not endorsed by, affiliated with, or operated by the FCC.
